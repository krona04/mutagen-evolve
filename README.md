## 📖 About

**Mutagen** adds 12 mob strains to Minecraft. Collect samples, research them in the lab, and transform through five stages. Each form has its own abilities and weaknesses.

## 📦 Installation

Download the matching `.jar` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/krona04/mutagen-evolve/releases). Put it in `mods` on the client and, for multiplayer, on the server. Use the same Mutagen build on both.

**Required:** [Architectury API](https://modrinth.com/mod/architectury-api), [GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib), and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) *(Fabric only)*.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Craft a **Bio Workbench** and use it to make your lab equipment.
2. Use a **Syringe** on a mob and study its sample in the **Sequencer**.
3. Purify a sample in the **Centrifuge** and make a serum in the **Synthesizer**.
4. Use the serum to start transforming. Press `G` to open the **Gene Tree**.

See the [wiki](https://krona04.github.io/mutagen-evolve/wiki/) for strains, abilities, and crafting recipes.
