Gameplay

From sample to serum

Collect a sample

Approach a mob in Minecraft and use a syringe to gather a sample.

Research the strain

Use the Sequencer to unlock stronger serums. Press G to open the Gene Tree.

Purify the sample

Process it in the Centrifuge to obtain a purified genome.

Make a serum

Combine a genome, mutagen base, and catalyst in the Synthesizer.

View illustrated recipes

Transformation

Five stages of mutation

  1. IInfection1–20%

    The first passive trait appears.

  2. IIManifestation21–40%

    Patches of the mob spread over your skin.

  3. IIIMutation41–70%

    Your body starts changing shape.

  4. IVDomination71–99%

    Flying strains gain flight. Handless forms lose the off-hand.

  5. VFull form100%

    You take the mob’s form. The change stops decaying.

Your progress can decay between doses. Once you reach full form, use a reversal serum to turn back.

Installation

  1. Use Java 21 and a Fabric or NeoForge profile for Minecraft 1.21.1.
  2. Install Architectury API and GeckoLib. Fabric also needs Fabric API.
  3. Put the matching files in the profile’s mods folder.

Pehkui is optional for body scaling.
