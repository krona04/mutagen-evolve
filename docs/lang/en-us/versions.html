Hotfix

Mutagen v0.2

Abilities & Transformations

  • FabricMinecraft 1.21.1Download .jar
  • NeoForgeMinecraft 1.21.1Download .jar
Changelog
  • Added nine strains for a total of twelve, with three active abilities per strain.
  • Added gradual creature models, new movement, creature vision, and an updated HUD and Gene Tree.
  • Added flight at stage IV for blaze, phantom and bee, plus updated restrictions for handless forms.
  • Extended the datapack format to support active abilities, vision, and new traits. Added a guide for datapack authors.
  • Added GeckoLib as a required dependency, expanded localization to 20 languages, and fixed missing damage modifiers.
