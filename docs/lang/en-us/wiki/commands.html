    Controls and commands

    Player shortcuts and operator commands for testing a strain.

    On this page
    • Player controls
    • Operator commands

    Player controls

    KeyAction
    GOpen the Gene Tree
    NToggle creature vision
    RUse the stage II ability
    VUse the stage III ability
    BUse the apex ability

    Operator commands

    The command group requires permission level 2. Strains accept an ID such as mutagen:zombie or the short name zombie. Brackets enclose optional arguments.

    CommandAction
    /mutagen status [target]Show strain, progress and other state.
    /mutagen listList available strains.
    /mutagen dose <strain> [quality]Add a dose while bypassing the normal research cap. Quality ranges from 1 to 100.
    /mutagen set <strain> <progress>Set transformation progress from 0 to 100.
    /mutagen lock <true|false>Enable or disable the progress lock.
    /mutagen clearClear active strains.
    /mutagen researchShow research progress.
    /mutagen research <strain> <percent>Set research for a strain.
    /mutagen abilitiesShow the active abilities and cooldowns.
    /mutagen use <index>Try to activate an ability by index.
