    Gameplay · 0.2.1

    Lab and research

    The workbench, machines, ingredients and research thresholds.

    On this page
    • Lab equipment
    • Research thresholds
    • Samples and catalysts

    Lab equipment

    MachineWhat it does
    Bio WorkbenchOpens a recipe list and lays out ingredients from your inventory when you select a recipe.
    SequencerConsumes a sample and redstone to advance research for that species.
    CentrifugeUses furnace fuel to refine a raw sample into a genome in about ten seconds.
    SynthesizerCombines a purified genome with mutagen base and a catalyst. A genome with at least 85% purity normally yields two ampoules.

    Research thresholds

    ResearchUnlock
    25%View the genes and make serum capped at 40% transformation.
    50%Make serum capped at 70%.
    75%Make serum that can reach full form.
    100%The strain holds without a stabilizer and can use its apex ability at full form.

    Samples and catalysts

    Use a syringe on the mob in Minecraft. Sample quality matters: adult or weakened targets tend to give better samples, and spawner mobs give poorer ones. Golden and netherite syringes improve sample quality. Some strains use a catalyst specific to that mob.

    Press G to see the strains you have sampled and their research progress. Mutagen shares research across the world by default.
