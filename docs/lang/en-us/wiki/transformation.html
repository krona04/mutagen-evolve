    Gameplay · 0.2

    Transformation

    How progress changes the player model and unlocks new traits.

    On this page
    • Progress and decay
    • The five stages
    • Stabilize or reverse

    Progress and decay

    An in-game serum dose fills an absorption reserve. Progress rises as the reserve is used and can fall again when it runs out. Full form is permanent until reversed.

    The five stages

    StageProgressWhat happens
    I. Infection1–20%Nothing you can see yet, apart from a few particles and the first passive trait
    II. Manifestation21–40%Patches of the creature start showing on your skin. First ability
    III. Mutation41–70%Your body starts changing shape. Second ability
    IV. Domination71–99%You're more creature than human. Flyers can fly, handless bodies lose the off-hand
    V. Full Form100%You are the creature. This stage doesn't wear off

    Stabilize or reverse

    Use a stabilizer to hold the current level. Use it again to release the lock. A reversal serum rolls progress back by 15 percentage points per dose and applies nausea, weakness and damage.

    At later stages the body shape, hitbox and camera height follow the creature. fullFormRender controls the creature renderer at 100%.
