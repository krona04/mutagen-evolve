    • Flies: double-tap jump. Flight burns hunger; rain and water ground you

    • Hollow bones: a frail body
    • Wings tear through any chestplate
    Bee
    • Pollen: crops and saplings nearby grow faster
    • Flies: double-tap jump. Flight burns hunger; rain and water ground you
    • Hovers down: falls never hurt
    • Lives on sweetness: honey, berries, fruit
    • A small body holds little blood
    Wolf
    • Runs like a wolf
    • Fangs bite hard
    • A wolf's stomach: raw and rotten meat is fine
    • Eats only meat
    • Llamas are terrifying
    • Vulnerable to fire
    Piglin
    • Piglins take you for their own — gold or no gold
    • Nether-tough hide: fire hurts half as much
    • Brute strength
    • Gold greed: won't throw away anything golden
    • Dreads the zombified: zoglins and zombified piglins
    • Away from the Nether the body weakens
    Guardian
    • Aquatic body: fast, buoyant swimming, gills and clear underwater vision
    • Spikes hurt whoever strikes you
    • Armoured scales
    • Needs water to breathe; air drains and vision becomes murky on land
    • Flops on land: much slower

    Ability keys and costs

    R unlocks at stage II, V at stage III. The apex ability on B needs full form and 100% research. The HUD shows cooldowns and unmet conditions. A failed attempt spends nothing.

    Blaze, phantom, and bee can fly from stage IV by double-tapping jump. Flight uses hunger, and rain or water grounds them.
